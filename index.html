01

Many index providers construct their indices with a fixed number of constituents, as exemplified by FTSE, S&P, and Deutsche Börse guidelines. This fixed-constituent approach, however, places a heavy burden of trust on economists and investors regarding the index provider’s selection decisions.

许多指数提供商在构建其指数时都采用固定的成分股数量，例如富时（FTSE）、标普（S&P）和德意志交易所的编制指南。然而，这种固定成分数量的方法，将巨大的信任负担推给了经济学家和投资者。

02

When such an index is intended to serve as a proxy for the overall market performance, the subjective choice of which assets to include—and which to exclude—becomes a critical point of contention.

当这样的指数被用作整体市场表现的代理指标时，哪些资产应当纳入、哪些应当排除，这种主观选择便成为争议的核心。

03

Investors must essentially rely on the provider’s judgment without a transparent, data-driven rationale for the constituent count, which can undermine confidence in the index’s representativeness.

投资者实际上只能依赖提供商的判断；如果成分股数量缺乏透明且以数据为依据的解释，指数的代表性就可能受到质疑

04

In contrast, the CRSP index family, designed for the US equity market, adopts a fundamentally different philosophy by not imposing any fixed limit on the number of constituents.

05

Instead, CRSP reviews and adjusts the constituent set daily until the selected members cover a predefined share of the total market capitalization.
